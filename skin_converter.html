TEXTURE SLOTS

CHANNELS

  • _R maps: red = roughness, green = metalness.
  • _N maps: OpenGL normals (Y+, green up). The Skin set has no normal map.
  • _I maps: RGB = light colour, alpha = grey codes that tell the game what the light is for (see the alpha table in the official thread).
  • Glass_D: colour tints the glass, brightness sets how opaque it is.
  • Skin_CoatR is optional: without it the clear coat is derived from roughness and metalness.
  • Don't bake ambient occlusion in, the game adds its own.

IN GAME

  1. Export the skin .zip (DDS textures + Icon.tga).
  2. Put it in Documents\Trackmania\Skins\Models\CarSport.
  3. Open your club → Skins → Upload a skin and pick the zip.
  4. In the skin menu, pick it with the arrows and fill the heart, then select it in your profile.
